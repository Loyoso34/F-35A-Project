Controls

  • Stick (left): pitch and roll
  • Right slider: throttle — afterburner (AB) at the top
  • Bottom row, left to right: flap lever → stick → rudder slider (center)
  • Rudder slider: drag to yaw; it springs back to center. On the ground it also steers the nosewheel
  • Flap lever: slide down to extend, up to retract. It snaps to each detent and shows the setting and flap angle
  • Landing Gear / Brakes: tap to toggle
  • Takeoff: you start with the brakes released and flaps up — open the throttle and rotate at about 140 kt
  • ☰ Menu: Pause, Camera (free → cockpit → chase → flyby → left wing → right wing → landing gear), Sound and Lights
  • Keyboard: WASD/arrows pitch and roll, Q/E rudder, Shift/Ctrl throttle, G gear, F flaps, B brakes, L lights, C camera, P pause
  • Two airports: Anadolu Air Base and Yesilova Airport. Choose your departure airport in the hangar
