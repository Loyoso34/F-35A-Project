Controls

  • Stick (left): pitch and roll
  • Right slider: throttle — afterburner (AB) at the top
  • Bottom row, left to right: flap lever → stick → rudder slider (center)
  • Rudder slider: drag to yaw; it springs back to center. On the ground it also steers the nosewheel
  • Flap lever: slide down to extend, up to retract. It snaps to each detent and shows the setting
  • Gear / Brakes: tap to toggle. FIRE (Space): AIM-120, wing stations first, then the internal bays
  • Takeoff: brakes released, flaps up — full throttle, start the rotation at about 150 kt; she lifts off near 180 kt (about 155 kt with flaps LAND). Raise the gear in the climb
  • Fuel: about 13 minutes at full afterburner, an hour at military power. With empty tanks the engine flames out
  • ☰ Menu: Pause, Camera (free → cockpit → chase → rear → flyby → cinematic → wings → landing gear), Sound and Lights
  • Keyboard: WASD/arrows pitch and roll, Q/E rudder, Shift/Ctrl or +/− throttle, G gear, F flaps, B brakes, L lights, C camera, R look ahead, Space fire, P pause, H this help
  • Airport: Milas–Bodrum (LTFE). Choose runway 10L or 28R and the time of day in the mission preparation screen
