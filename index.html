Controls

  • Left stick: pitch and roll
  • Right slider: throttle — afterburner (AB) at the top
  • Rudder slider (bottom centre): drag it; it springs back to centre. On the ground it steers the nosewheel
  • Landing Gear / Flaps / Brakes: toggle on and off. Spoilers A321neo only
  • Take-off: release the brakes, full throttle, rotate at about 140 kt
  • ☰ Menu: Pause, Camera (free camera → cockpit → chase → flyby → left wing → right wing → landing gear), Sound and Landing Lights are in this drawer
  • Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Q/E rudder, Shift/Ctrl throttle, G gear, F flaps, V spoilers, B brakes, L lights, C camera, P pause
  • A321neo: the flap lever steps 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → FULL; on the ground the brake also engages reverse thrust
  • Two airports: Anadolu Air Base and Yesilova Airport. Pick your departure point on the selection screen
